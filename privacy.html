Privacy notice

Your colors stay yours.

Effective September 23, 2026 · Version 1.2.0

Local processing

The extension reads webpage DOM and computed CSS colors to replace colors according to rules you select. This happens in your browser. When you use the screen eyedropper, the selected color also stays local. We do not receive page content, selected pixels, browsing history, or palettes.

Settings stored on your device

Chrome local extension storage holds source and target HEX colors, similarity thresholds, rule IDs, profile names, hostnames, and enable switches. No account is required. The extension has no backend, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud synchronization.

Permissions

Temporary current-tab access is used when you invoke the extension. Persistent access is requested for sites you choose so saved rules can apply on later visits. You can optionally grant access to all regular HTTP and HTTPS websites. Browser-protected pages cannot be remapped.

Sharing and export

The extension does not transmit or sell your data. Export creates a JSON file at your request; you control any subsequent sharing of that file. A profile export can contain the hostnames you configured.

Control and removal

You can edit or delete profiles, reset stored settings, revoke website access in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Reset does not revoke Chrome permissions; those can be managed separately. Reload already-open pages after revoking site access to remove existing content scripts.

Contact and updates

For support, use the publisher contact on the Chrome Web Store listing. Any future feature that sends data outside the browser will require an updated privacy notice and explicit consent.
